# Short Form Reels

Category: media.

## Steps
1. Pick one idea and one takeaway.
2. Write a 1 to 2 second hook (question, bold claim or visual surprise).
3. Script 15 to 45 seconds: hook, 3 quick beats, payoff, call to action.
4. Plan shots and on-screen text for silent viewing.
5. Add trending-safe audio and captions.
6. Write caption, 3 to 5 hashtags and posting time.

## SELECT check
If an installed skill, plugin or connected tool does this job better (real generator, editor, connector), use it and keep these steps as the quality gate.
